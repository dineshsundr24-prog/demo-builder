Staff web portal for Gulf Crest Bank branch and contact-centre staff.

1. Dashboard: KPIs (open service requests, KYC reviews due, new accounts this week, complaints), chart of requests per week, table of latest requests.
2. Customer Search: table of customers with search and filters by segment and emirate; open the customer.
3. Customer 360: profile (name, CIF, segment, risk rating), relationship KPIs, contact and KYC details, products table, actions (raise service request, update KYC).
4. Service Requests: queue with status and date filters, open a request.
5. Request Details: request information, timeline, documents.
6. KYC Review: customer identity details and documents, decision (approve, request more information, reject) with comments; the status updates.
7. Reports: KPIs, monthly chart, exportable table.
