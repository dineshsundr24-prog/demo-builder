Falcon Bank wants a mobile app demo focused on cards and personal finance for UAE retail customers.

Screens:
1. Home: greeting, balance of the current account, quick actions (Cards, Loans, Pay bills, Transfer), recent transactions.
2. My Cards: show the Platinum debit card, freeze and unfreeze, online and international usage controls, daily spending limit, change PIN, replace card.
3. Personal Finance Offer: product features, an instalment calculator (AED 10,000 to 500,000, 12 to 48 months, 5.99% flat), eligibility (salary AED 8,000+, UAE resident), Apply now.
4. Finance Application: employer, monthly salary, purpose, requested amount and tenure, salary certificate upload, consent.
5. Review Application: summary of what the customer entered and the calculated instalment, confirm.
6. Application Submitted: reference number, next steps, back to home.
7. Pay Bills: DEWA, Etisalat, du and Salik, upcoming bills, consumer number, amount, pay.
